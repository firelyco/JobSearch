Connect Yahoo job-alert emails

JobSearch checks LinkedIn alerts during its daily 6 AM America/New_York refresh. This runs in the cloud; your computer can be off.

  1. Open Yahoo Account Security. Find App passwords, generate a new app password named JobSearch, and keep it out of chat and source code. This is separate from your normal Yahoo password. Yahoo instructions.
  2. Open JobSearch → Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions. Add repository secrets YAHOO_EMAIL (the full email address receiving alerts) and YAHOO_APP_PASSWORD (the generated app password).
  3. By default the reader checks INBOX. If alerts are filed elsewhere, add a repository variable JOBSEARCH_ALERT_MAILBOX with the exact IMAP folder name. Only that folder is checked.
  4. In LinkedIn, enable daily email delivery for each relevant job alert. App-only notifications are not included. LinkedIn alert settings.
  5. Run Poll job boards → Run workflow to test, or wait for the next daily refresh. On the dashboard open LinkedIn job-alert coverage to inspect counts and original links.

The reader uses Yahoo IMAP over TLS, selects the mailbox read-only, and fetches with BODY.PEEK. It does not send/delete messages or mark them read. An app password itself is not a read-only permission; read-only behavior is enforced by this app. Revoke the app password in Yahoo to disconnect.

Message identifiers and scan progress are stored in your private database. Raw bodies, recipient addresses, attachments and credentials are never published or passed to the assessment model. The public report contains job references and aggregate scan counts.

The first scan covers 14 days, with 200 new messages per run. Backlogs and failures remain visible and are retried on subsequent refreshes. Employer-link resolution uses up to 10 public search queries per run. Similar titles are possible matches, not proof of the same requisition. Missing employer links remain unresolved until verified, rather than receiving a fit verdict.
